01 / The product opportunity

The forecast arrives.
The decision is still hers.

Buying seeds and preparing land commit money before the season is certain. A regional warning becomes useful when a farmer can connect it to a specific crop and the water she actually has.

Kavach combines local conditions, seasonal outlooks and applicable agricultural guidance into a small set of choices, explained in the farmer’s language.

Our first question: “What should I consider before sowing chickpea in Latur with limited irrigation?”
Start with one district and crop. Prove the decision flow before expanding.

03 / What exists in the product

One useful flow.
Three layers of capability.

Farmer experience

Understand. Decide. Revisit.

  • Short, confirmed farm profile
  • Separate water and heat indicators
  • Prioritized, conditional action card
  • Questions in familiar language
  • Sources and uncertainty on demand
Decision foundation

Evidence becomes applicable guidance.

  • Historical and recent data adapters
  • Consistent regional baselines
  • Crop-stage and water constraints
  • Reviewed recommendation rules
  • Reproducible assessment snapshots
Expert operations

Keep the advice worth trusting.

  • Guidance review and versioning
  • Freshness and feed health checks
  • Unsupported-case review queue
  • Farmer feedback and corrections
  • Later: assisted FPO/KVK workspace
The design principle

AI explains.
Reviewed rules guide decisions.

The conversation collects context and makes a structured result understandable. Measurements, action eligibility and supporting references come from the decision service.

Code alone does not make an assessment correct. Rules need agronomic review, evidence needs validation, and unsupported cases need an honest next step.

04 / The foundation

Three kinds of evidence.
One traceable answer.

Keep past conditions, recent estimates and future outlooks distinguishable. Match the detail of the claim to the resolution of the data.

01 · Collect

Rain, moisture, heat, forecasts and applicable guidance.

02 · Normalize

Dates, units, geographic boundaries, versions and quality.

03 · Assess

Transparent indicators and reviewed crop-specific rules.

04 · Explain

Plain language, source links, uncertainty and next steps.

Product questionCandidate inputsBoundary we must respect
What has happened here?CHIRPS rainfall · ERA5-Land · MODIS vegetationComparable baselines and historical coverage; greenness is not crop loss.
What are conditions now?SMAP moisture · latest weather observations where availableShow actual freshness; regional models do not measure an individual field.
What may happen next?Short-term weather · seasonal ensembles · NOAA ENSO contextSeasonal outlooks support area-level uncertainty, not exact sowing dates.
What action applies?CRIDA contingency plans · current IMD advisories · confirmed farmer inputsCheck applicability and freshness; review rules with a qualified agronomist.

Proposed stack: Earth Engine batch processing → BigQuery regional history → Cloud Run decision API → Gemini explanations. Operational storage holds farm contexts and dated plans. See the source and API register ↗

05 / Where we fit

A focused decision workflow
in an existing ecosystem.

FarmerChat already offers local-language agricultural conversations. Cropin and ClimateAi offer agricultural or climate intelligence. IMD publishes weather-based agricultural advisories.

Our hypothesis: farmers and advisers need an easier way to match seasonal evidence to a concrete crop decision, inspect the reasoning and revisit the plan.

Distribution proposal: begin with an FPO or KVK-assisted pilot. Evaluate partner channels before assuming farmers will discover and adopt a standalone app.

No partner integration or commercial access is secured. Read the competitive review ↗

06 / Build in evidence-backed increments

Start small enough
to validate the whole decision.

First / prove usefulness

Latur × chickpea

Confirmed context, real dated evidence, curated applicable guidance, reviewed rules, action card, text follow-up and source drawer.

Exit: an expert can reconstruct and assess every recommendation.

Next / prove usability

A plan farmers understand

Validate Marathi/Hindi voice, add save/share and plan refresh, build reviewer tooling, then expand crop and district coverage.

Exit: farmers understand the conditions and can identify their next step.

Later / prove delivery

Reach through trusted channels

Opt-in alerts, assisted FPO/KVK workflows, WhatsApp/IVR, institutional APIs and reviewed region packs.

Exit: reliable access, repeat use and measured decision changes.

Measure what we have demonstrated. Start with evidence traceability, expert-rated applicability and farmer comprehension. Avoided losses require a comparative field study; they are not a prototype outcome.
