## Recommended position

Follow-up: [opportunity gap review](opportunity-gap-review.md) adds MahaVISTAAR-AI, Fasal and DeHaat and distinguishes documented adoption barriers from unvalidated product differentiation.

Kavach helps a rain-dependent farmer make a particular seasonal crop decision under uncertain water availability, explains evidence and alternatives, and revisits the plan as conditions change. Demonstrate that workflow rather than claiming to be the first agricultural chatbot or climate-risk platform.

Differentiation hypotheses to validate: official guidance matched to explicit farmer constraints; inspectable evidence; uncertainty that farmers understand; persistent plans with justified revisions; assisted delivery through FPOs/KVKs.

## Corrections to the original brief

- Do not assert that a competitor has no seasonal-risk layer from absence of public documentation.
- Voice and location-specific advice are existing capabilities, not sufficient novelty claims.
- Named partnerships and integration access remain proposals.
- The supplied 2026 monsoon statistics, market sizing, impact ratios and hackathon requirements were not all independently verified in this research pass. Verify their exact source, date and context before external use.
