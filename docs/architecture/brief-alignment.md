### Protection scope and remaining brief gaps

The state PMFBY calendar is now implemented for all three selected crops, gated behind explicit contextVersion 2: rabi jowar 30 November 2026, gram and irrigated wheat 15 December 2026. These are published policy dates, not verified personal deadlines. Village notification, portal amendments, category, documents and bank participation must be checked. No current coverage claim or insurance enrollment is made. See the method document for page citations and freshness rules.

This closes the missing source-backed insurance-calendar workflow; it does not complete the brief. Still open: crop-masked district vegetation, current official storage linked to actual access, calibrated crop-outcome risk, current agronomically reviewed actions and external farmer/native-speaker validation. Maps/BigQuery are implementation choices requiring a real product need and cost review, not substitutes for those evidence gaps. Recent-chat persistence is explicitly deferred after the owner's POC scope decision.
